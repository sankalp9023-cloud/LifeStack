# LifeStack — Project Overview

## 1. Vision & Purpose

**LifeStack** is a unified personal productivity and life-management ecosystem designed to bring diverse aspects of personal progression into a single, cohesive, futuristic digital environment. 

Rather than fragmenting life management across disjointed tools for tasks, study tracking, fitness, and scheduling, LifeStack consolidates these domains into an orchestrated "command center" powered by an intuitive and visually striking neural-inspired interface.

## 2. Core Modules & Roadmap

### Phase 1: Initial Scope
1. **Command Dashboard**
   - Central neural brain visualization representing real-time cognitive activity and overall system status.
   - Surrounding orbital glassmorphism widgets displaying life metrics, focus state, active priorities, and recent activity.
2. **Study Management**
   - Subject and topic categorization.
   - Active study session trackers (Pomodoro, deep work timers).
   - Flashcards, notes, and retention analytics.
3. **Project Management**
   - Multi-tier task and milestone organization.
   - Kanban / list / timeline perspectives.
   - Project health metrics and progress trajectories.

### Phase 2: Extended Scope (Future Modules)
4. **Fitness Management**
   - Workout routines, exercise logs, recovery metrics, and nutritional targets.
5. **Event Management**
   - Smart agenda, milestone scheduling, and temporal coordination with personal goals.

## 3. Guiding Principles

- **Focus & Clarity:** Eliminate visual clutter; highlight vital signals over noisy data.
- **Biophilic & Neural Aesthetics:** Natural, curved silhouettes and neural-inspired visuals rather than rigid, cookie-cutter boxes.
- **Performance & Fluidity:** Instantaneous state transitions, reactive visual feedback, and smooth 60fps micro-interactions.
- **Modularity:** Independent, decoupled feature modules that scale without creating monolithic friction.
